Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining the Sanctum.

How do I join Arcanum Sanctum?
Register through MEXC (code: mexc-sanctum) or Bitunix (code: SANCTUM), deposit minimum $50, then submit the verification form on our portal. Once verified, you gain full access to all Sanctum resources including signals, courses, mentoring, and rewards.
What is the Divination Chamber?
The Divination Chamber is our active crypto trading education hub covering ICT concepts, market structure, order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity, risk management, live sessions, and premium futures signals. Designed for traders who want to develop consistent execution from beginner to advanced.
What is the Atlas Chamber?
Atlas Chamber covers long-term investing and professional trader development. This includes ETF investing (VOO, QQQM, SCHD, VXUS), portfolio building, DCA strategies, CFD trading, and prop firm challenge preparation through Atlas Funded and Alpha Capital Group.
How do the prop firm partnerships work?
We partner with Atlas Funded and Alpha Capital Group. Atlas Funded provides funded accounts after completing their challenge. Alpha Capital Group offers 40% off all accounts with code SANCTUM. These are separate from community verification and can be accessed independently.
Is this suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. Both chambers have dedicated beginner paths starting from absolute zero. No prior trading or investing experience required. The Sanctum takes you from wherever you are and builds systematically with structure, mentorship, and community accountability.
What ETF platforms work for Philippine investors?
We recommend GoTrade for beginners — supports GCash, BPI, and Maya deposits, zero commission, fractional shares from $1. Use referral code 4GLD90. For more serious investors, IBKR GlobalTrader offers professional-grade access with $0 commission on US ETFs.
How do I contact the team?
Reach us through Discord, via email at pattss.core@gmail.com, or through our social media channels. For partnership and collaboration inquiries contact pattss.core@gmail.com or kimbeansent11@gmail.com.
