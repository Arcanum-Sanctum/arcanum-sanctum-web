Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers instantly. Can’t find what you need? Our team is one message away.

What is Arcanum Sanctum?
Arcanum Sanctum is a premium trading and investing community built for traders who want to develop real, long-term skills. We provide crypto signals, ETF education, forex and TradFi analysis, prop firm access, and structured mentorship — all within a single community ecosystem.
How do I join Arcanum Sanctum?
Join our Discord server at discord.gg/Mp8Fhsmd5S. To unlock full access to the Divination Chamber, register on Bitunix using code SANCTUM and make a minimum $30 deposit. To unlock the Aether Chamber, purchase a funded account through Atlas Funded, FundedTraderMarkets, or AquaFunded using code SANCTUM. Then submit your verification on our portal.
Is Arcanum Sanctum free?
You can join our Discord server for free and access public channels. To unlock the Divination Chamber, you need to register on Bitunix and deposit a minimum of $30. To unlock the Aether Chamber, you need to purchase a funded account through one of our official prop firm partners.
Is Arcanum Sanctum suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. Both the Divination Chamber and Aether Chamber include dedicated beginner paths that start from absolute zero. No prior trading experience is required. The community is built to take you from wherever you are and develop you systematically with structure, mentorship, and accountability.
How does Sanctum verification work?
Visit our verification portal and choose your path. For Divination Seeker, register on Bitunix using code SANCTUM, deposit at least $30, then fill out the form and upload a screenshot showing your UID and deposit. For Aether Seeker, purchase an account through one of our official prop firm affiliate links, then upload your receipt. Our Sentinels review every submission and assign your role within 24 hours.
What is the difference between Divination Seeker and Aether Seeker?
Divination Seeker is for crypto traders who register on Bitunix. It unlocks the Divination Chamber, which covers ICT concepts, crypto signals, live sessions, and active trading content. Aether Seeker is for CFD and prop traders who purchase a funded account. It unlocks the Aether Chamber, which covers prop firm challenges, CFD trading, ETF investing, and professional trader development. You can hold both roles by completing both requirements.
What do I need before submitting verification?
For Divination Seeker: a registered Bitunix account using referral code SANCTUM with at least $30 deposited, your Bitunix UID, your Discord username, and a screenshot showing your account and deposit. For Aether Seeker: a completed purchase through our official prop firm affiliate link, your Gmail address, account type and size, Discord username, and a copy of your purchase receipt or order confirmation.
How long does verification take?
Our Sentinels typically review submissions within 24 hours. Make sure you are already in the Discord server so your role can be assigned as soon as your verification is approved.
What should I do if my verification is rejected?
Check that your proof clearly shows all required details — for Divination Seeker, both your UID and deposit of at least $30 must be visible; for Aether Seeker, your receipt must show your name or email, account details, and that code SANCTUM was applied. Resubmit through the verification portal with a clearer screenshot or receipt. If the issue persists, contact a Sentinel directly on Discord.
What should I do if I cannot access a chamber after verification?
First, confirm you are in the official Arcanum Sanctum Discord server. If your verification has been approved but your role has not been assigned, message a Sentinel on Discord with your verification submission details. If you have not yet received a confirmation, your submission may still be under review — allow up to 24 hours before following up.
Where can I find trading signals?
Trading signals are posted in the Divination Chamber, which is accessible to verified Divination Seekers. After completing verification, your role will be assigned in Discord and you will gain access to the signals channel and all related content.
What markets does Arcanum Sanctum cover?
We cover Crypto Futures (BTC, ETH, SOL, and more), Forex pairs, Gold (XAU), Silver (XAG), Indices, Commodities, ETFs (VOO, QQQM, SCHD, VXUS), and US Stocks. The specific markets available depend on which chamber you are verified for.
Are the signals guaranteed to be profitable?
No. Trading involves significant risk and past performance does not guarantee future results. Signals are educational and informational. Always apply your own risk management and never risk more than you can afford to lose. Arcanum Sanctum does not provide financial advice.
How do I use the Bitunix partnership?
Visit bitunix.com and register using our referral code SANCTUM, or use our official affiliate link on the Partners section of this site. After registering, deposit a minimum of $30. This qualifies you for a 20% trading rebate and allows you to submit for Divination Seeker verification.
Where can I find the current partner discount offers?
All current partner offers are listed in the Partners section of this website. Use code SANCTUM at each partner platform to activate the discount. Links on this page are always official affiliate links — do not use links from unofficial sources.
What discount do I get on Atlas Funded?
Atlas Funded offers 50% off all funded accounts when you use code SANCTUM through our official affiliate link. This is the largest discount available through our partnership. Always use the link on our Partners page to ensure the discount is applied correctly.
What discount do I get on FundedTraderMarkets and AquaFunded?
FundedTraderMarkets offers 40% off using code SANCTUM. AquaFunded offers 40% off Instant Accounts using code SANCTUM. Use the official links on our Partners page to ensure your discount is applied at checkout.
Can I hold both Divination Seeker and Aether Seeker roles?
Yes. You can hold both roles by completing both requirements separately — register on Bitunix with the $30 deposit for Divination Seeker, and purchase a funded account through one of our prop firm partners for Aether Seeker. Each grants independent access to its respective chamber.
What happens after I am verified?
After verification is approved, a Sentinel will assign your role in Discord. You will immediately gain access to the corresponding chamber and all its content — signals, courses, mentorship sessions, analysis, and community channels. If your role is not assigned within 24 hours of submitting, message a Sentinel on Discord.
What is the Sanctum Rewards system?
Verified members can earn points through community engagement and activity. Points can be redeemed for vouchers, funded account slots, and exclusive Sanctum access. Details are shared within the verified channels in Discord.
How do I contact support?
The fastest way is through our Discord server at discord.gg/Mp8Fhsmd5S — tag a Sentinel or post in the support channel. For formal or detailed inquiries, email us at pattss.core@gmail.com. You can also use the Support button at the bottom right of this page to find contact options or submit a support request.
What information should I provide when reporting an issue?
Include your Discord username, the type of issue (verification, access, payment, technical), a clear description of what happened and what you expected, any relevant reference numbers or order IDs, and a screenshot if applicable. The more detail you provide, the faster we can resolve your issue.
The verification form is not working. What should I do?
Try refreshing the page or using a different browser. Make sure your file size is under 10MB and is in PNG, JPG, or PDF format. All required fields must be filled before proceeding. If the issue continues, contact us on Discord and we will process your verification manually.
I submitted verification but received no confirmation. Did it go through?
After a successful submission, you are redirected to a confirmation page. If you did not see that page, your submission may not have gone through. Contact us on Discord with your details and we will check our records and guide you from there.
