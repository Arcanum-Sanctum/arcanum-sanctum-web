Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Arcanum Sanctum (“we”, “us”) operates this website and the Arcanum Sanctum trading education community. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the website, why, and what choices you have. We operate from the Philippines, and we handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

1. Information we collect

Verification submissions. If you complete the Sanctum Verification form we collect:

  • your chosen path (Divination Seeker or Aether Seeker) and the partner platform;
  • for the Divination path: your exchange UID and Discord username;
  • for the Aether path: your Gmail address, account type, account size and Discord username;
  • the proof image you upload (a screenshot or receipt), which may contain account details you choose to show; and
  • the date and time of your submission.

Analytics (only with your consent). If you accept analytics, Google Analytics collects usage information such as pages viewed, device and browser type and approximate location. On the verification and confirmation pages, a Whop analytics script also runs when you accept. If you choose “Essential only”, neither is loaded.

Technical and contact data. Our hosting provider and the services below may process standard technical data such as your IP address and browser details when you load the site. If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply.

2. Why we collect it

  • To verify that you meet the requirements for the Divination or Aether chamber and to assign your Discord role.
  • To keep records of verifications, prevent abuse and handle support requests.
  • To understand how the website is used, where you have agreed to analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We rely on your consent, on carrying out the verification you request, on our legitimate interest in running and protecting the community, and on legal obligations, as applicable.

3. Where it is stored and who receives it

  • Google: verification submissions are sent through Google Apps Script to Google services we use to record them (including Google Sheets). Google Analytics is used for analytics if you consent. Google Fonts is loaded when you open a page, which sends your IP address to Google.
  • Whop: analytics on verification pages, if you consent.
  • Discord: we use your Discord username to find your account and assign roles. Discord handles your account under its own policies.
  • Partner sites: links to exchanges and prop firms take you to independent sites with their own privacy policies. We do not control them.
  • Hosting provider: serves the website to you.

We do not sell your personal information. Some of these providers process data outside the Philippines.

4. How long we keep it

We keep verification records only as long as needed to administer your access, keep reasonable records and handle disputes. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see section 7), unless we must keep it to comply with the law.

5. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and restricted access to the records we hold. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not include more information in a proof image than the verification requires, and hide details that are not needed.

6. Children

This website and community are intended for adults. They are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may, subject to its conditions, be informed about how your data is processed, access it, object to processing, correct it, ask for its erasure or blocking, receive a copy in a usable format, claim damages for unlawful processing, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, email 0xkazzz@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details of the cookies and similar technologies we use and how to change your choice.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
